1: When Angels blow their trumpets,
All the dead shall resurrect,
Those who lie in righteousness,
Shall behold Christ on that day.

2: When Gabriel shall arrive,

With songs of happiness,
Father count us worthy,
That we might see Christ on that day.

3: Michael our conqueror,
Descend with thy mighty sword,
And conquer for us,
That we may behold Christ that day.
Amen
