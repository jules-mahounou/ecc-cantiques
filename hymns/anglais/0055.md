1: Worship, worship the Lord God,
Worship Him devotedly,
Worship, worship the Lord God of life.

2: He is the King of king
That speaks from abode great beam of
light
Worship, worship the Lord God of
light.

3: Chant ye, chant ye Hossanah,
Hossanah is our victory,
Chant ye, chant ye Ho-Ho-Ho-
Hossanah.                                 Amen.
