I accept Jesus Christ as my King,
Jesus is the most gracious King,
My mind is at rest with the Lord,
He will never let me down,
Chorus: Throughout my life,
He will never let me down,
In this holy fold,
He will never let me down,
In my family,
He will never let me down,
Among enemies,
He will never let me down.         Amen
