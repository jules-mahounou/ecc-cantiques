Jesus here we come to thy holy home,
Come into our midst and bless us all,
For we are children and we know
nothing,
Place thy hand on us and pray for us,
For we are sinners and we know
nothing,
Place thy hand on us and grant us thy
mercy
Forgive us our sins and sanctify us,
Place thy hand on us and draw us nearer
to Thee.                                      Amen
