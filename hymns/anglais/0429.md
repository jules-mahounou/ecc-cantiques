1: Labour ye on, and seek ye no repose,
Keep on sowing seeds, the harvest now
cometh,
The eyes of the Lord are set on your
works,
Chorus: Holy Angels are now recording
them,
Be it good or be it evil,
Thou shall be rewarded accordingly.

2: You stand aloof from working,
When others are busy working,
The eyes of the Lord are set on your
works,
Chorus: Holy Angels are now recording
them,
Be it good or be it evil,
Thou shall be rewarded accordingly.

3: At this instant, the trumpet keeps
sounding,
Put in the sickle, the harvest is ripe,
No more prejudice mercy as of old,
Chorus: Holy Angels are now recording
them,
Be it good or be it evil,
Thou shall be rewarded accordingly.
Amen
