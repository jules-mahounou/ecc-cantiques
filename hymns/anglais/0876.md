1: It is Me don’t be afraid,
I say am the King,
The King of providence,
Do no be afraid,
I shall always provide your needs for
thee,
Lift up thy eyes to the sky and count the
stars,
And see if you can count the number.

2: And thus shall be the number
Of the children to come,
Do not be distressed,
For I shall provide,
Joy and good blessing  for thee,
For I the King who understands the
desires of man,
I the King of blessing shall bless thee.
Amen
