1: Ye beloved
I have come to abide with you
Come closer with joy.

2: Behold the Heavenly Glory
That the host of Angels
Descended for you today.

3: O ye children of My Church
Do My wishes
That the joy may manifest.

4: O ye My choristers
Behold the joy with great glory,
That is given to you today

5: O ye beloved
Sing songs in love
To earn you crown of life.

6: Behold the shining glory
To the entire world
Let the glory shine

7: The glory of Heaven descended
Upon the children
To abide with you today.

8: O ye Celestial Church
Behold the work that was assigned
To you spiritual children.

9: Behold the choristers
That sing Holy to the the King of life
Prove worthy to be with them.

10: Be careful and watchful
Of the glory given you
That it may last forever.

11: Halleluyah is the song
That the host of Angels sing
To praise the Father above.

12: Behold the glorious crown

That the host of Angels
Descended for you children

13: Take care of your position
That have been provided for you
That you may inherit life

14: The position shall be reserved
That is preserved for us
That you may inherit life eternal. Amen
