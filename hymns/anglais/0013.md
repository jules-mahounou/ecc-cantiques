1: Good behaviour for thee all I pray
O my entire prophets
Eschew all bitterness and envy
And move nearer to the Lord.

2: Holy Jihmata gives this message
That ye all rejoice and rejoice,
Clear your minds from all sinful deeds
That you may move nearer to Christ.
Amen
