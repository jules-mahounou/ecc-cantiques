Lets go and adore Him in Bethlehem,
Where He was born,
Lets go and adore Him in Bethlehem,
Where He was born,
Burst into joy, burst into joy,
With heavenly host of Angels,
Burst into joy, burst into joy,
With heavenly host of Angels,
The heavenly stars burst into joy,
For the Saviour who came to take away
the sins of  the world,
Ye host of heavenly Angels,

Burst ye for joy with the holy stars
above.                                      Amen
