The children dance, they all rejoice
While Father also rejoice,
With songs of joy.

2: Halleluyah, we shall sing,
While Angels also rejoice,
With songs of joy.

3: Holy, Holy,
We shall all sing,
At that time King of Glory
Glorify us.                             Amen
