1: Raise ye up song of praises,
Chanting Halleluyah,
Sing ye all songs of praises,
His glory magnify.

2: Descend heavenly Spirit,
And strengthen us we plead,
Giving the world assurance,
That thou art our God,.

3: Holy, Holy, Holy,
Angelic voice sing
Praising our Father on high,
Maker of Heaven and Earth.      Amen
