1: Celestial Church repent of sins,
The bridegroom comes very soon,
If He shall meet us on the last day,
That we shall behold His glory.

2: Celestial Church be full of joy,
The bridegroom comes very soon,
If He shall meet us on the last day,
So that we might behold His glory.
Amen
