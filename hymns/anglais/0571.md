1: What will be our song,
The day we will see Jesus,
A! A! A! A! Halleluyah.

2: How full our joy will be
The day we will see Jesus,
A! A! A! A! Halleluyah.

3: How well shall we dance,
The day we will see Jesus,
A! A! A! A! Halleluyah.

4: Keep your handwork
To partake in the joy
A! A! A! A! Halleluyah.          Amen
