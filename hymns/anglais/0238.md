1: Holy Spirit descend in our midst,
As authorized by Father in Heaven,
Your work cometh, quickly come
down,
Show unto us all your ways,

Holy Spirit the Spirit of life,
Descend upon us your children,
So we may praise Thee fervently,
At this moment and forever.

3: Holy Spirit, Spirit from Heaven,
Father, Son and Holy Spirit,
Endow this Church with Thy great
power,
That the living Church be full of praise.
Amen
