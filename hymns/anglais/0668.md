1: In my Father’s house in heaven
above,
Very many mansions exists there,
Jesus is there, Angels are there,
Singing Holy Halleluyah,
Halleluyah Holy, Halleluyah Holy,
Halleluyah, Halleluyah, Halleluyah.

2: Time to sound last bell draws near,
That the whole world will congregate
To receive compensations,
According to their labour,
On my part I’ll do good, on my part I’ll
do good,
I will do good to receive salvation.
Amen
