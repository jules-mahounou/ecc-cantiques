1: Celestial Church let us go,
To the said holy land,
The holy land, the safe land,
The holy peaceful land.

2: O ye the world bow with reverence
To the said holy King,
Our God the everlasting King,
Of the holy heaven.

3: Let all us be prepared
To worship the King
The King who rules above the earth,
The everlasting King.                 Amen
