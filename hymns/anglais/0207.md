Golden organs sound loudly,
Round the Heavenly court-yard,
Melody is all the songs,
That the Angels are singing,

With great and lound praises,
They bow down their heads,
With  great humilty,
They give glory to King of Heaven
Glory, glory, praises to Father
Maker of the earth and of Heaven,
Halleluyah, Halleluyah
Hossanah is their song,
Halleluyah, Halleluyah
Hossanah sound is their song.  Amen
