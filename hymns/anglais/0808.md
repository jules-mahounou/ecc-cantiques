1: Father, the Son, the Holy Ghost,
Thou art our Lord shall engage us
whole heartedly,
It is You Father who calls us,
And we hearken to Thee.

2: Jesus Christ is coming soon,
To give judgement to the world,
Holy Michael the captain,
Shall ride the horse to the earth.

3: With seven blazing swords,
And a deadly spear at hand,
Horses shall trample on human beings,
The end of time has arrived.   Amen
