1: Our Father who dwells with us,
Guide us not to shame,
In Thy hands thou keep us safe,
Abide with us to the end.

2: Heavenly Spirit, descend,
And work with us we plead,
Those who worship devotedly,
Will be saved on that day.

3: Door of mercy open wide,
For those thou called with grace,

Work that ye might gain heaven,
Your rewards are stored there.

4: The day of joy now cometh,
That the Saviour cometh in,
The Angels are filled with joy,
For that great glorious day.         Amen
