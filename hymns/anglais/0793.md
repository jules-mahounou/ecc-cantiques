1: Blessing, blessing cometh,
My door open,

For  blessing to enter.

2: The joy, the joy cometh,
My door open,
For joy to enter.

3: The life, the life cometh,
My door open,
For the life to enter.

4: Who is, the giver of life?
But only Jesus
Our Redeemer.

5: Fear not all the evil ones,
O ye Celestians,
Be strong in mind.                     Amen
