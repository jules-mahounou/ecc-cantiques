1: Come and be with us, our Father,
Come and be with us,
We lift up our eyes unto the Lord,
Our good Father,
Come and be with us, our Father,
Come and be with us.

2: Look on us with mercy,  our Father,
Look on us mercifully,
Kindle your shining-light round us,
And show the whole world,
Look on us with mercy,  our Father,
Look on us mercifully.

3: Fight against Satan, Michael,
Fight against Satan,
We shout and rebuke Satan
To flee from us
Fight against Satan, Michael,
Fight against, Satan.

4: Keep us steadfast in this Church,
Keep us steadfast,
Kindle your shining-light round us,
And show the world,
Keep us steadfast in this Church,
Keep us steadfast.                    Amen
