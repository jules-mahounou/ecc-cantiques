1: O all ye the Heavenly Host,
Our Lord and Father in Heaven,
Holy Spirit enter our heart,
Holy, Holy, Holy, Holy.

2: O all ye the Heavenly Host,
Holy, Holy, Father in Heaven,
Father divine, purify us,
Wretched sinners, Father in Heaven.

3: O ye all the Heavenly Host,
Holy, Holy, from Heavenly above,
Holy, Holy, the Angels sing,
Holy, Holy, Holy, Holy.            Amen
