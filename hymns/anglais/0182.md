1: Happy are ye for this great day of
resurrection
God hath spare our lives till this day
Thanks to God who spares our lives
till this day
Our joy, joy, in abundance
In union with the Hosts of angels.

2: Rejoice, rejoice, in the Lord
Shout majesty to the most High
Jesus Christ is the King of today
Rejoice, rejoice for our salvation
That is given to the world in Jesus
Blood.

3: Shout Hosanna to our King
Redeemer of the world risen
Jesus Christ is the King of today
Halleluyah unto God,
That giveth His Son for world
salvation.

4:  Celestial Church bow down to
worship,
The saviour of world is risen
The heavenly stars glorify Him
He is made King, Jesus is King of
the world
worship Jesus in unity.        Amen
