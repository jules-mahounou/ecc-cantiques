Sing ye aloud the songs of the Lord,
With the host of Angeles in heaven
above
Sing ye sweet songs in harmony.

2: Ye beloved in Celestial Church,
Magnify the Lord in heaven above,
With the host of Angeles in heaven
above
Praise ye the Father on His throne.
