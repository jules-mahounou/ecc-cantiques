1.
To zonlinzin to oyise mẹ
To gigo to whanpẹ Kluno ton mẹ
To afo dé di i yiseno
Na nukun Kluno ton pinpon we

Bẹji:
To ayajẹ, to gigo yi
To wanyi 'Kluno t'we ton mẹ
Na whanpẹno wẹ Kluno miton
Nutẹgbe lẹpo ni to pipa-E

2.
Hlan mi mẹhe 'ylan tukla do le
Mi mehe to ogbojẹtẹn din lẹ
Di wamono nukuntonno lẹ
Mi na mo whlẹngan to Klisti de
Bẹji: To ayajẹ, to gigo yi ...

3.
Jesu Aholu to yiylo we
Etè so wẹ a to te nopon
Wa dù bo nù t'ayaje ton mẹ
Na gbigbo towe ni t'ayajẹ
Bẹji: To ayajẹ, to gigo yi ...
