1.
Jiyẹwhe Aholu sie,
Whiwhẹ mẹ yen sẹpo we,
Jlo na m'ayajè yi' whé
Okluno Mẹdato sie

2.
To yise mẹ yen bio we,
Gigo daho lon mẹ ton
To yise ma hanu mẹ
Ma yin ayiha-awe mẹ

3.
Gbigbo wiwé lon ton wâ
Jète wâ na huhlon mi
N'aihon nido yonẹn do
Hiẹ wẹ Jiyẹwhe miton

(Niṣẹ)
