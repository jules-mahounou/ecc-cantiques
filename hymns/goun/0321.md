Yẹn ma do nu de,
Etẹ yẹn tindo
Adavo jomion, yen mo yise mẹ
Bẹ awagun dido po goyi
Se lẹpo son jié
Ylanno n'yin

Bẹji:
Ylandono he mo
Whlẹngan yi
Gbon jomion vonu
Mẹ yẹn yin
Gigo hlan Mawu
Mẹdato wanyino
Ylanno he mo whlẹngan
Wẹ yẹn yin

(Niṣẹ)
