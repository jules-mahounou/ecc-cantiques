1.
Jiwheyẹwhe 'Hol' hinhonnọ
Holu madopodo
Gigo sisẹ he hugannọ
to'mehe nukọn,
Pẹvi de t'oylan lẹ mẹ
Ma sogan sepo gbede,
Yi wanyi do ylo ylannọ
Nado mo gigo'Tọn

2.
Mẹhe jlo Klunọ wẹ-'E no ylo,

Zinvlu ton he mẹ
Aihon gbẹ ylo lo bo
Zonlinzin to zinvlu mẹ
Ma tin lẹnpon bo vojo to Husüdo lo glọ

3.
Hiẹ mẹh yan son pa-Eden mẹ

Notẹn wanyi tọn

T'ofin Kluno ton nukọn
