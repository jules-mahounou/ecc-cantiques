1.
Gbigbo wiwe jẹte
Pagbe godo ton wẹ
He Klisti do wẹpo do yi
Lon Oto Eton de

2.
Gbigbo wiwe jẹte
Homẹjladoto wa
Gbigbo wanyino Nugbono
Mi to nukun do we

3.
Gbigbo wiwe jẹte
D'agùn Wiwe t'we ji
Hẹn miwulẹ zun 'zonwàto,
Nugbono tenoglino

4.
Apoe 'lon ton jẹte,
Pon madogan miton
Yi gbigbo m'ton do basi notẹn
Bo tin po mi po

5.
Dopo to aton mẹ
Wiwe we oyin T'we
Na huhlon gbigbo Wiwe ton na
Mi n'do wà 'zon lo

6.
Gbigbo Wiwe jète,
Aton to dopo mẹ
Oto, Ovi gbigbo Wiwe
Wâ se dègbe miton

(Niṣẹ)
