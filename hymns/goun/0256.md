1.
Mẹde mono klo Oyẹn gba
Oyẹn Jesu we to modo
Eyin hiẹ gbè po to ylan lo kon
Agbàn he-ado wẹ hiẹ na hẹn

2.
Donano wẹ medepope
He to budisi Okluno
He zinzonlin t'ali-Eton lẹ ji
Na-e na dù-ale azon alo-eton ton

3.
Donano wẹ hiẹ naso yin
Ena so to yinyon na we
Asi towe na tayi di ovẹn
Sinsẹn na to owhé towe gbe

4.
Ovi sunnu t'we lẹ na di
Atin olivi ton lẹdo we
Hiẹ na so mo dàgbe lẹpo
To azân ogbè towe ton mẹ

(Niṣẹ)
