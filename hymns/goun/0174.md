1.
Owhé po osun po sunwhlẹvu-emi
Angẹl' lon Wiwe ton lẹ Emi,
Mi nopo pa Okluno lo
N'agùn wiwe' lon mẹ ton he jète
Nado bẹ lẹngbovu lẹ yi Oto nukon

2.
Azé p'owon po na li t'ai
Lẹgba na dahli t'agùn he nukon
Wẹnsino na mo ho bo ji
Oku-ajiji ma na tin ba
Huhlon gbigbo Wiwe ton na kon do mi mẹ

3.
To Donamẹto wa do na mi,
Dona huhlon gbigbo Wiwe ton
Dona azon jijoho ton
Po dona jijoho ton po
Ni kon d'agùnvi he lẹ ji
T'aṣè To ton mẹ

(Niṣẹ)
