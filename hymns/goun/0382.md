1.
Yẹn jlo na yin T'we
Okluno to 'gbèzân sie mẹ
Nado hodo We
Gbon fi he A degbè na mi
Yẹn jlo na yin T'we
Okluno to 'gbèzân sie mẹ
Na hunhlon mi
Ma sogan wà jlo Towe

2.
Yẹn jlo na yin T'we
Okluno to 'gbèzan sie mẹ
T'alogo T'we mẹ
Ma gan tin na pipa Towe
To Oklé t'ozan
Ma sogan mo gigo Towe
Okluno dagbeno
Yẹn to vivẹ We

(Niṣẹ)
