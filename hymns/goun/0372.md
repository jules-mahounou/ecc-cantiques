1.
T'afonnufudamẹ Bo whenu dangboji
Gbemu ton to sise Bo-ahün t'amaji
Fọn p'ayajijè po Bẹ ayigbado sè
Go-alo najibẹto Bẹ nọkun wa whé

Bẹji:
Bẹ nọkun wa whé, Bẹ nọkun wâ whé
Whẹn' ogbè vivi ohè ton to nudo
Sé ogbé Jesu ton, Bo hun hô-Eton gbapé
Go-alo najibéto, Bẹ nọkun wa whé

2.
Whenu 'whè to vive, Bo-ayi miton hunhun
Bo afojẹhon yin, Bo-ama to kiko
To whejai damẹ, Bo hinhọn gbigba pé
Go-alo najibèto, Bẹ nọkun wâ whé
Bẹji: Bẹ nọkun wa whé ...

3.
Eyin 'hu to-adan jẹ, Bo-asisà 'gbowhẹn fon
Yin-aihon to hunhun,Tôdidô de tin,
Aigba dagbe na hẹn, Sinsén dagbe ton kun
Go-alo na jibẹto, Bẹ nukun wa whé
Bẹji: Bẹ nọkun wa whé ...

(Niṣẹ)
