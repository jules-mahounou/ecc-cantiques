1.
Owhé, Osun, hinhon
Po sunwhlẹvu, mi li t'ai
T'Ogan Gigonọ lo nukon,
Yẹyi asovo mẹwiwé lẹ ton To hinhon ta
To Didato Olon ton nukon
Mẹ he gigo yẹyi, pipa jẹ hẹ

2.
Gbedide Ton mẹ
Ye da nutẹgbẹ mi d'E Holu,
Bo jihàn vivi yajẹ ton
Ohàn he angẹli,
Lon ton lẹ jiji to 'lon mẹ
Gbon wanyi Whlẹnganto Jesu Klisti
Ton ton mẹ hlan ovi gbèto ton

(Niṣẹ)
