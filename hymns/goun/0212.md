Jesu a jo mi do blo
Yawu go-alo bo whlẹn vi t'we lẹ
Adiké mi ylo we whè he glo bo
To godo mẹ mi mo wiyan blo

Bẹji:
Whla mi do yè t'we glo
Bo whlẹn devi towe lẹ
Zé mi do adusilo t'we mẹ
Na jẹgbakun ni yin miton

(Niṣẹ)
