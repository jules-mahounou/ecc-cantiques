1.
Mi jihàn fonsonku ton Halleluyah
N'angẹl' lon ton lẹ yi hàn lo do Halleluyah
Jesu-klist gba 'whan kü ton Halleluyah
Lon po-aihon po ni béji do Halleluyah

2.
Jesu klist hun paladise Halleluyah
Vo wẹ ku po lonzodo po Halleluyah
Opẹ wẹ na mẹflito lo Halleluyah
Mi jihàn mẹfligo ton do Halleluyah

(Niṣẹ)
