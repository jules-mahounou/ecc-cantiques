1.
Pon azon he a ylo mi na
Pon ali he ji a ylo mi do
Jesu, Jesu, Jesu gbawhàn na mi

2.
Pon azon he a ylo mi na
Pon ali he ji-a ylo mi do
Jesu, Jesu, Jesu wâ whlẹn mi gan

3.
Pon azon he a ylo mi na
Pon ali he ji a ylo mi do
Jesu, Jesu, Jesu zé mi d'aga

4.
Pon azon he a ylo mi na
Pon ali he ji-a ylo mi do
Jesu, Jesu, Jesu wâ dona mi

(Niṣẹ)
