1: Maleka Mimọ,
Wọn nt’Ọrun sọkalẹ wa,
Nipa ore ofẹ Olugbala,
Wọn nyi itẹ Rẹ ka.

2: St, Gabriel onibode,
O n forin ayọ yin wọn,
Ẹ jẹ ka kọ orin mimọ,
Ọrin ọpẹ f’Oluwa.

3: Ẹ jẹ ka f’ọkan wa ṣọkan,
F’ọpẹ f ‘Olugbala,
Nitori t’Oluwa mbẹ,
Pẹlu wa nigba gbogbo.

4: Maleka oniye mẹfa
Wọn nfi meji wọle,
Wọn nfi meji boju,
Wọn nfi meji yin Oluwa.                 Amin
