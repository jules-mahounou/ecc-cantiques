2: Sunmọ mi pẹlu Ẹmi mimọ,

Ma jẹ ki nlọ lọwọ ofo,

3: Maikeli mimọ ode-Ọrun
Wa sọ ẹru mi ka lẹ,
Gba mi Ajagun-sẹgun.             Amin
