1: Imọlẹ, Imọlẹ, eyi t’owurọ,
Eyi t’owurọ, o tan si asalẹ,
Chorus: Halleluya, Halleluya Ijọ Mimọ,
Ẹ yọ, imọlẹ na ni Ijọ yi.

2: Irawọ, Irawọ eyi t‘owurọ,
Eyi t’owurọ, o tan si asalẹ,
Chorus: Halleluya, Halleluya Ijọ Mimọ,
Kọrin iyin si Ọba Ọrun.

3: Gbogbo aiye, gbogbo aiye,
Ẹkun fun ayọ fun ‘jọ ikẹhin yi,
Chorus: Halleluya, Halleluya
Ijọ kẹhin bori,
Halleluya f’Ọba.                      Amin
