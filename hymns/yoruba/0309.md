1: Mo yin Ọba mi,
Mo yin Ọ loke,
Ẹ gbe Orukọ Rẹ ga titi lai.

2: Ẹ yin Ọba Imọlẹ,
Ẹ wolẹ niwaju Rẹ,

Ẹ bere Orukọ Rẹ,
Yio fi fun wa.

3: Ẹ wo Ọba Imọlẹ,
Ti mbẹ pẹlu wa,
Yio wa pẹlu wa dopin aiye.         Amin
