1: Ẹ gbe orin iyin soke,
Ẹ ke Halleluya,
Ẹ gbe orin iyin soke,
Ẹ gbe Ogo Rẹ ga.

2: Ẹmi ọrun sọkalẹ,
Wa fun wa lagbara,
Kaiye le mọ daju pe,
Irẹ li Ọlọrun wa.

3: Mimọ, Mimọ, Mimọ,
Lorin awọn Angẹli,
Fi yin Baba wa loke,
Ẹlẹda ọrun on aiye.                       Amin
