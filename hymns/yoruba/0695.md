1: Nigba Angẹli fun ipe,
Awọn oku yio ji dide,
Awọn to sun lododo,
Yio ri Jesu l’ọjọ na.

2: Nigba Gabrieli ba de,

Pẹlu orin ayọ,
Baba ka wa mọ wọn,
Ka le ri Jesu lọjọ na.

3: Michael Balogun wa,
Sọkalẹ pẹlu ida rẹ,
Lati sẹgun fun wa,
Kale ri Jesu lọjọ na.                           Amin
