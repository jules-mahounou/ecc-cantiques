1: Nile Baba mi l’oke ọrun,
Ọpọ ibugbe lo wa nibẹ,
Jesu wa nibẹ, Angẹli wa nibẹ,
Wọn nkọ Mimọ Halleluya,
Halleluya Mimọ, Halleluya Mimọ,
Halleluya, Halleluya, Halleluya

2: Agogo kẹhin fẹrẹ dun,
Ti gbogbo aiye yio pejọ,
Ta o pin fun olukaluku,
Gẹgẹ bi iṣẹ ọwọ rẹ,
Emi yio ṣe rere, emi o se rere,
Emi yio ṣe rere, ki nle ri ‘ye.         Amin
