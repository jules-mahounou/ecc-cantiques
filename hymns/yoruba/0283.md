1: Ẹ wo gbogbo araiye,
Ohun Ẹlẹda yin,
Ọjọ ‘dajọ sunmọ le,
A o ba yin siro,
Chorus: Gbogbo aiye lo ṣẹ,
Ti wọn si kuna Ogo Rẹ,
Ẹlẹsẹ orukọ Jesu yio gba wa la,
Ko si ọna miran to tọka s’ọrun,
Ẹyin araiye, ẹ yara wa gba Jesu.
