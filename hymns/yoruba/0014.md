Mimọ, Mimọ, L’Ọlọrun wa,
O boju Rẹ wo sara awa ẹlẹsẹ
Iyanu nisẹ Rẹ gbogbo,
Mimọ, l’Ọlọrun wa Mimọ,
Pẹlu itẹriba l’awọn Angẹli nkọrin
Orin iyin didun,
Baba, Ọmọ, Ẹmi Mimọ,
Ore-ọfẹ Rẹ gbogbo pọ,
Lori Ijọ Mimọ.                          Amin
