1: Aiye, ẹ gbe orin yi,
Orin Mimọ t’Ọrun
Aiye, ẹ gbe orin yi,
Ẹ kọrin s’Ọba Oluwa.

2: Ẹyin Ijọ Mimọ,
Ẹ korin s’Ọba Oluwa,
Ẹ wo ade ti Baba,
Sọkalẹ f’Ọba Alade,

3: Ẹyin Ijọ Mimọ,
Ẹ gba ade yi silẹ,
Awọn to yan falade,
To ṣe tan lati sin.

4: Aiye mo ti ṣe tan,
Lati ṣe nyin logo,
Ẹni to ba mura,
Ogo na ti ṣe tan.

5: Ijọ Mimọ temi,
Lat’Ọrun lo ti wa,

Awọn to sin nibẹ,
Ninu imọlẹ ‘wọn wa.

6: Awọn Angẹli ni,
Ṣe ransẹ Mi logo,
Iransẹ ti aiye yi
Ẹ mura lati sin.

7: Imọlẹ na ṣe tan,
O si ntan si aiye,
Awọn to wa lokunkun,
Nwa sinu Imọlẹ.                     Amin
