1: Emi a yin Jesu l’ogo,
Emi a yin Jesu l’ogo,
Ore to ṣe fun mi pọ,
Emi a yin Jesu l’ogo.

2: Emi a yin Jesu l’ogo,
Emi a yin Jesu l’ogo,
Ẹni to ku fun mi lori agbelebu,
Emi a yin Jesu l’ogo.

3: Emi a yin Jesu l’ogo,
Emi a yin Jesu l’ogo,
Ẹni to pese fun jije ati mimu,
Emi a yin Jesu l’ogo,

4: Emi a yin Jesu l’ogo,
Emi a yin Jesu l’ogo,
Ẹni to mu mi la wahala aiye yi ja,
Emi a yin Jesu l’ogo.                    Amin
