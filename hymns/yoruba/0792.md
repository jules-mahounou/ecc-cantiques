Gbadura, gbadura, gbadura, gbadura,
Gbadura s’Ọba imọlẹ,
K’Ọba Mimọ jọwọ
Lati wa mu leri mi ṣẹ,
Gbadura, gbadura, gbadura, gbadura,
Gbadura s’Ọba Mimọ.                 Amin
