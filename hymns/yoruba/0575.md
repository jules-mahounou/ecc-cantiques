1: Oluwa ma fi mi ṣe gbagbe,
Ranti mi to ba de jọba Rẹ,
Nibi igbala on ayọ gbe wa,
Nibi igbala aiyeraiye.

2:  Oluwa to loke to ni ilẹ,
On lo l’Ọrun to laiye,

Bo ti wu kile aiye dun to,
A o faiye silẹ nigbakan.

3: Nigbati Maria bi Jesu,
Awọn amoye wa,
Gbogbo wọn wa fori balẹ fun,
Nwọn si nta Jesu lọrẹ.                   Amin
