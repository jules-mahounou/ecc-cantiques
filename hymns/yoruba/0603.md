1: Baba, Baba Ọrun,
Lo pasẹ fun ‘jọ yi,
Awamaridi ni,
Isẹ Oluwa wa.

2: Ijọ Mimọ, ẹ yọ,
Ninu ‘sẹ Baba yin,
To logo Mẹta, ni,
Ogo f’orukọ Rẹ.

3: Aiye yio si wa yọ,
Ninu agbo Kristi yi,
Agutan ti Baba,
Yio kuro ninu egbe.               Amin
