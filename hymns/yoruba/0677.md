1: Ade Mimọ temi ti pese,
F’awọn to gba Mi gbọ,
Awọn to gba gbọ yio d’ade na,
Lati sogo ikẹhin.

2: Aiye ikẹhin yio ti dun to,
F’awọn to leti igbọ,
Awọn to leti igbọ yio wa,
Nipo ayeraye.                                   Amin
