O yẹ wa, ka f’ọpẹ fun Ọba Oluwa,

Ẹ jẹ k’a wa f‘ọpẹ fun Oluwa,
Era ilẹ wọn dupẹ,
Yarin okun wọn dupẹ,
Ẹ jẹ ka wa na dupẹ,
Halleluya f’Oluwa,
Ẹ jẹ ka yin Oluwa.                   Amin
