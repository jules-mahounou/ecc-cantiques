1: Ọrun, Osupa, Imọlẹ, Irawọ,
Ẹ wolẹ niwaju Ọga Ogo,
Ogo ara wọn maleka mimọ
N’tan mọlẹ niwaju Ẹlẹda Ọrun
T’iyin ọla, ọpe, ogo yẹ fun.

2: Nipa ọrọ Rẹ l’ada efufu,
E se l’ọba ẹ kọ orin ayọ didun,
T’a gbe  si yin l’ẹnu,
Ẹnyin Angẹli,
Mimọ t’ọrun nipa ifẹ Olugbala
Jesu Kristi si ọmọ araiye.             Amin
