1: Ẹyin araiye, Ẹyin araiye,
Ẹ gbe Jesu ga, ẹ gbe Jesu ga,
Jesu Baba Ijọ Mimọ,

Jesu Baba Ijọ Mimọ,
To joko sori ọwọ ọtun,

2: Ẹ ko ẹsẹ nyin, ẹ ko ẹsẹ nyin,

Wa si jọ’ Mimọ
Jesu Kristi, ore ofẹ,
Jesu Kristi, ore ofẹ,

T’awọn Angẹli foribale,
